# IGS 

Ishita Gallery Store (IGS)

**Business Name:** Ishita Gallery
**Type:** Online Gift Shop

**Description:**
Ishita Gallery is an online gift shop where customers can explore and buy a variety of gifts. The website should be modern, user-friendly, and visually appealing. It must be dynamic, allowing easy updates to products and billing information.


**Website Features and Structure:**

1. **Home Page:**

   * Attractive hero section with featured products or offers
   * Eye-catching banner image representing gifts
   * Call-to-action buttons like "Shop Now" or "Explore Gifts"

2. **Products Section (Cards Layout):**

   * Display products as cards with images, name, price, and "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" buttons
   * Filter options (by category, price, etc.) if possible
   * Responsive design for mobile and desktop

3. **Billing & Checkout Section:**

   * A user-friendly checkout form
   * Fields like name, address, contact, payment options
   * Order summary and confirmation

4. **About Us Page:**

   * Description of Ishita Gallery and its mission
   * Images of the store, products, or events
   * Team introduction or story behind the brand

5. **Reviews Section:**

   * Display customer testimonials with names and ratings
   * Option to add new reviews if possible

6. **Navigation Bar:**

   * Links to Home, Shop, About Us, Contact, and Reviews
   * Sticky or easily accessible across all pages

7. **Header:**

   * Logo and name "Ishita Gallery"
   * Contact details or shopping cart icon
   * Search bar if applicable

8. **Footer:**

   * Contact information, social media links, newsletter signup
   * Links to privacy policy and terms & conditions

9. **Additional Sections:**

   * Offers or discounts section
   * Newsletter signup
   * FAQs or support section

**Design Style:**

* Clean, modern, and vibrant
* Easy to navigate
* Optimized for fast loading and mobile responsiveness

**SEO & Performance:**

* Ensure meta descriptions, alt texts for images, and optimized URLs
* Make it easy to share products on social media

**Tone:**

* Friendly, helpful, and trustworthy
* Encourage users to explore, shop, and connect with the brand
